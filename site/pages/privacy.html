Privacy policy

Last updated: {{updated}}

This policy explains how {{company}}, {{address}} ("we") handles personal data in {{brand}} Cloud, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.

1. Two kinds of data

Account data: what we collect about you to run your account. For this we decide why and how it is used.
Customer Data: the traces your application sends (inputs, outputs, context and metadata). Your organisation decides what is sent and why; we process it only to provide the Service on its instructions. If Customer Data contains personal data of your users, your organisation is responsible for informing them and, where needed, getting their consent.

2. Account data we collect

  • Name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash) and organisation name.
  • Billing details: plan, payment status and the ids our payment providers give us. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's or Stripe's pages; we never see or store them.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser type for sign-ins, security and rate limiting.
  • Messages you send us.

3. Why we use it

To create and secure your account, provide the Service, process payments and issue invoices, send service emails (confirmations, invitations, usage and billing notices), answer support requests, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not send marketing emails without your consent, we do not sell personal data, and we do not use Customer Data to train AI models.

4. Personal data in traces

Before Customer Data is stored, the Service replaces email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, API keys and IP addresses with placeholders. This reduces, but does not remove, personal data in what you send; please send only what you need.

5. Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai, India. We share data only with service providers who help us run the Service: Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage, email), Razorpay (payments in India) and Stripe (international card payments, which may process data outside India). Separately, if the public demo's live-model option is enabled, the question after email, phone, IP, card and key redaction, plus selected synthetic help-center text, is sent to the Ollama endpoint configured by the demo operator. Do not enter secrets in the public demo. We may disclose data where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Customer Data is kept for your plan's retention period (shown on the pricing page) or until you delete it or your organisation. Account data is kept while your account is active and then up to 12 months, except billing records, which we keep as long as tax law requires. Backups expire within 35 days.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, encrypted storage, a separate database per organisation, hashed passwords and keys, role-based access and audit logs. See the security page.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, complete or erase your personal data, withdraw consent where we rely on it, and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot. Many of these you can do yourself in Settings (change your name, export or delete your organisation). Otherwise email {{email}}. For personal data inside Customer Data, contact the organisation that sent it to us; we will help them respond.

9. Grievances

Grievance officer: {{grievance}}. We respond within the time set by law. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

11. Changes

We will post any change here and email account owners about material changes before they apply.
