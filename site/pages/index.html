How it works

Questions

What exactly do you store?

The traces your app sends: inputs, outputs, retrieved context, model names, tokens, latency and cost, after personal data is redacted. You choose what to send; you can delete it any time.

Do you train AI models on my data?

No. Your data is used only to show you your traces and run the checks you ask for. LLM jobs run on our own GPU server, never on a third-party AI provider.

Which languages and frameworks work?

The Python SDK is one file that needs only the standard library. Any other language can send JSON to the REST API with one HTTP call.

What happens when I reach my plan's limit?

We email you at 80% and at 100%. At 100% new traces are refused until the month ends or you upgrade; your app keeps working because the SDK never blocks it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid month, then you move to the Free plan. See the cancellation and refund policy.
