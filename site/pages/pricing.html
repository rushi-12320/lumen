Billing questions

How do I pay?

In India: UPI, cards and net banking through Razorpay. Outside India: international cards. Plans renew monthly until you cancel.

Do I get a GST invoice?

Yes, for every payment. Send your company's GSTIN to {{email}} and we will show it on your invoices so you can claim input tax credit.

Is there a free trial of Team?

Yes, 14 days, once per organisation, with no card needed. When it ends you move to the Free plan unless you upgrade.

What if I go over the limit?

We email you at 80% and 100% of your monthly traces. Above 100% new traces are refused until the next month; nothing is charged automatically.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel any time in Settings > Billing; the plan stays active until the end of the paid month. See the cancellation and refund policy.
