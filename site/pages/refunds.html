Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: {{updated}}

Cancelling

You can cancel a paid plan at any time in Settings > Billing, or by emailing {{email}}. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Your organisation keeps the paid plan until the end of the period already paid for, then moves to the Free plan. Nothing else is charged.

Refunds

  • First payment: if {{brand}} is not right for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund of that payment.
  • Duplicate or wrong charges: refunded in full.
  • Service failure: if the Service was unavailable for a significant part of a paid period because of a fault on our side, ask us and we refund the affected part.
  • Otherwise, payments for a period that has started are not refunded, but you keep access until it ends.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of approval. When your bank or card issuer shows it can take longer, depending on them. GST is refunded together with the amount it was charged on.
